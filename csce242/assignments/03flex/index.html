Crystal Sword

Weapon Information

The Crystal Sword is one of the weapons Cloud can use in Final Fantasy VII. Its design features a bright crystalline appearance that makes it stand out from many of his other swords. The weapon can be useful for players who want a strong balance between offense and special abilities. Like Cloud's other weapons, it can be paired with Materia to create different combat setups.

Weapon Abilities

Cloud's weapons are not only different in appearance, but they can also support different styles of combat. Some weapons focus more heavily on physical attack power while others can improve magic or defensive abilities. Weapons can also provide unique skills that Cloud can learn and continue using later. Choosing the right weapon allows players to customize Cloud based on the enemies and challenges they are facing.
