Acquisition Guide

Weapon Locations

Find out where Cloud can obtain weapons throughout his journey.

Buster Sword

Location: Starting Weapon

Cloud begins the game with the Buster Sword already equipped, so there is no special location or chest needed to obtain it.

The Buster Sword is Cloud's signature weapon and is designed as a balanced option for both physical and magical combat. Its weapon ability is Focused Thrust, which is useful for increasing an enemy's stagger.

Crystal Sword

Location: Weapon Shop

The Crystal Sword can be found inside a purple treasure chest while exploring the Gongaga Reactor during Chapter 9.

This weapon gives Cloud strong magical power while still keeping good physical attack strength. Its weapon ability, Infinity's End, delivers a powerful overhead strike that deals especially high damage to staggered enemies.

Rune Blade

Location: Mid-Game Area

The Rune Blade is found inside a purple treasure chest while progressing through Junon during Chapter 4.

The Rune Blade focuses heavily on magic and is a good choice for players who use Cloud as more of a spellcaster. Its weapon ability, Disorder, lets Cloud attack while switching between his combat modes.

Igneous Saber

Location: Later Game Area

The Igneous Saber is found inside a purple treasure chest while traveling through Upper Mt. Nibel during Chapter 11.

This weapon focuses strongly on physical attack power and is a good choice for players who want Cloud to deal heavy melee damage. Its weapon ability, Blade Burst, sends a wave of non-elemental mako energy toward enemies.

Sleek Saber

Location: Later Game Weapon Area

The Sleek Saber becomes available later in Cloud's journey. It is designed for players who want a fast and balanced weapon while still maintaining strong physical attacks.

Its lighter design makes it useful for quick combat while still giving Cloud enough power to handle stronger enemies.

Slipstream Saber

Location: Advanced Area

The Slipstream Saber can be obtained as Cloud progresses into more difficult areas of the game. It is built around speed and aggressive close-range combat.

This weapon is useful for players who prefer quick attacks and want to keep pressure on enemies during battle.

Umbral Blade

Location: Late Game Area

The Umbral Blade becomes available during the later parts of Cloud's journey. It is a powerful weapon designed for stronger enemies and more difficult battles.

Its focus on offensive power makes it a strong choice for players who want Cloud to deal heavy physical damage.
