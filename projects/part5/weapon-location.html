Weapon Locations

Find out where Cloud can obtain weapons throughout his journey.

Buster Sword

Location: Starting Weapon

Cloud begins his journey with the Buster Sword already equipped.

Crystal Sword

Location: Weapon Shop

The Crystal Sword can be obtained later in Cloud's journey as stronger equipment becomes available.

Rune Blade

Location: Mid-Game Area

A weapon suited for players who want a balance between physical attacks and Materia use.

Igneous Saber

Location: Later Game Area

A strong saber that becomes available as Cloud reaches more difficult areas.
