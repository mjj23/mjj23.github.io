Iconic Weapon

Buster Sword

The Buster Sword is Cloud Strife's most recognizable weapon. Its massive blade was originally carried by Zack Fair before eventually becoming Cloud's signature weapon.

Despite its enormous size, Cloud uses the Buster Sword with impressive speed and power. It represents Cloud's past, his connection to Zack, and his journey throughout Final Fantasy VII.

Weapon Type: Greatsword

Owner: Cloud Strife

Strength: Balanced

Materia Slots: 2

Combat Use

The Buster Sword is designed for powerful close-range attacks. Cloud can use it for standard attacks, defensive techniques, and abilities that deal heavy physical damage.
